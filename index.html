01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE SOUND

MEET ZADDY B.

From bedroom ideas to festival stages, the mission has always been the same: make music you can feel.

ZADDY B is a DJ and producer building his own sound, one set, remix and unforgettable night at a time. What started with playing a few tracks made with friends grew into a confident, high-energy style and opportunities to support incredible artists, perform in new cities and play his first festival set at Tahoe Live.

Driven by the crowd, the community and a love for creating, ZADDY B is bringing fresh music, big moments and even bigger energy into 2026.
